Privacy, in plain language.

Workspace Reflow is built so the sensitive thing—the content you read—does not need to leave your browser.

What the extension stores

The extension stores your selected site rules, reading preferences, and optional supporter license in your browser’s local extension storage. Rules contain a site origin, a DOM selector, a short label derived from the selected region, and your reading settings. They do not contain the region’s body text.

What it sends

Selected page content and preferences are never sent to us or a third party. If you enter a supporter license, the token is sent over HTTPS to the Sociobot license API no more than once per day to check whether it is active.

The website

This website uses no analytics, advertising pixels, third-party scripts, or remote fonts. It stores only your theme choice and, if supplied, your supporter license token and cached verification result in local storage. The static site can be served without cookies.

Your control

Remove a site rule from the extension popup. Remove all extension data by uninstalling the extension or clearing its storage. Remove website data through your browser’s site-data controls. You can use all accessibility features without buying or entering a license.

Contact and changes

Questions can be raised through the project’s public source repository. Material changes will be dated on this page. We do not sell personal data.
